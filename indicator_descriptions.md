# Orca Suite Indicator Descriptions

Here is a one-line description for each indicator in the suite, as well as the Trade/Risk Manager:

* **Orca Risk/Trade Manager**: Comprehensive on-chart risk manager for trade execution, position sizing, scaling, and dynamic P&L tracking (includes active tracking and calculator modes).
* **Orca Anchored VWAPs**: Anchored VWAP indicator with three separate timeframe tracking instances, ATR-based or tick-based reversal thresholds, standard deviation bands, and region fills.
* **Orca MGI Daily**: Plots structural market levels from current and prior daily sessions including overnight range, value areas, opening range, initial balance, VWAP, and half gap.
* **Orca MGI Statistics**: On-chart statistics panel displaying average ranges, volumes, value area widths, relative volume, and harmonic rotation levels.
* **Orca MGI Weekly**: Plots structural levels from current and prior weekly sessions including weekly initial balance, range, value area, and VWAP tracking.
* **Orca Rolling Profiles**: Continuous rolling volume and delta profile anchored to configurable structural time periods (minutes, hours, days) with visual Value Area and POC rendering.
* **Orca Time VWAPs**: All-in-one Anchored Time and Rolling VWAP utilizing Deviation bands and various anchor options.
* **OrcaAbsorptionCandles**: Paints standard candlesticks based on volume delta intensity (absorption) to visually highlight aggressive buyer/seller activity.
* **OrcaCandleVolumeProfile**: Custom footprint chart indicator that draws candles along with per-candle volume profiles featuring optional delta coloring and Value Area nodes.
* **OrcaCumulativeDelta**: Cumulative delta OHLC candles rendered on a separate indicator panel to track the net imbalance of trades at the bid/ask.
* **OrcaExecutionLines**: Automatic execution lines with FIFO round-trip matching, SQLite history, and integrated R-multiple analysis capabilities.
* **OrcaLegtoLegProfile**: Rotation-based leg delta/volume profile with Value Area, POC, mirror-mode arrangement, and gradient support.
* **OrcaStepProfile**: Time-based volume profiles at fixed temporal intervals with dual volume/delta histograms, intensity gradients, POC, and Value Areas.
* **OrcaTickDirectionIndex**: Tick Direction Index (Rewarded Effort Index) — mathematically tracks volume classified strictly by tick direction movement.
* **OrcaTimeStatistics**: Displays granular Time Statistics metrics (Volume, Delta, Delta Efficiency) persistently at the bottom panel of the chart.
* **OrcaVisualOrders**: Interactive drag-to-create graphical interface for placing and visualizing TP (Take Profit) and SL (Stop Loss) orders directly from the chart canvas.
